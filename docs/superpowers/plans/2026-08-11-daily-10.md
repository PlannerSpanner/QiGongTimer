# Daily 10 — 10-minute daily bodyweight + dumbbell timer app

New sixth page `daily-10.html`, built from app_tpl.js (full timer architecture: wall-clock
anchor, wake lock, voice, chimes) — NOT the wk reference template. Purpose: daily postural
maintenance alongside the Wed/Sat strength block; zero meaningful recovery cost; anterior
pelvic tilt correction is the primary theme.

## Constraints (go in the intro text verbatim-ish)

- AC joint separation, right shoulder: no pressing below parallel, no wide-grip pressing,
  no behind-the-neck anything; pulling volume > pushing.
- Distal bicep tendon rehab: all pulling neutral or pronated grip, never supinated.
- Right peroneal tendon: no jumping, no lateral bounding.
- Anterior pelvic tilt correction is a stated goal of the routine.

## Segment plan (600s total, exactly 10:00)

Transitions follow EVERY movement (10 gaps). `tdur` on a movement = duration of the gap
BEFORE it (default 10; 15 where the setup change is bigger: to/from the floor, dumbbells,
the bench, the wall). `setup` on a movement = authored voice line spoken at gap start.

| # | Movement | pos | work | gap before | why gap length |
|---|----------|-----|------|-----------|----------------|
| 1 | Dead bug | supine | 45 | — | session starts supine (intro says so) |
| 2 | Glute bridge | supine | 45 | 10 | stay supine, plant feet |
| 3 | Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch (bil) | kneeling | 60 | 10 | floor-internal change |
| 4 | Dumbbell bent-over row | standing | 45 | 15 | floor→stand + grab DBs |
| 5 | Bench dips (strict partial) | bench | 40 | 15 | DBs down, move to bench |

| 10 | Cat-cow | quadruped | 30 | 15 | stand→floor |
| 11 | Standing posture reset (br) | standing | 30 | 15 | floor→stand |

Work 465 + gaps 135 = 600s. The two bilateral stretches keep 60s (30/side); everything
else absorbed the trim, per the brief ("trim work intervals as needed").

## app_tpl.js changes (all opt-in; the four existing apps are bit-identical in behavior)

1. `blend()` gains swAL/swAR lerp lines (copied from wk_tpl.js) — arm IK swivels.
2. Prop system replaced by wk_tpl's `resPt`/`propShapes`: points may be world coords,
   joint names, `{j,o}` joint+body-frame offset, `{m:[..],o}` midpoints; shapes `{l}`,
   `{c}` (+`f:1` filled), `{db:[P,P,r]}` dumbbell. Joint-referenced props re-resolve every
   frame inside drawFig (existing world-coord props render identically).
3. `const PROPFIT=false` — when true (daily-10 build), fitOf includes prop extent in the
   bounding box (wk behavior). False preserves existing apps' framing exactly.
4. `const TR_ALL=false` — when true (daily-10 build):
   - SEGS gets a gap before EVERY movement i>0, `dur = m.tdur||10` (pos-tag rule unused);
   - gap voice line = `m.setup` (posCue() prefers m.setup);
   - figure during a gap holds the upcoming movement's A keyframe (ph=0, static), instead
     of looping the preview;
   - paint() toggles `.state-trans` on .wrap; injected CSS (X2OPT pattern) restyles the
     countdown + fill bar in prop-tan #b9a98b so a glance separates setup from work;
   - the 3s warning in a gap becomes a distinct triple tick (520Hz at 3/2/1) vs. the
     single 380Hz tick kept for work segments.

## Poses (fit numerically; research-anchored; verify in renders)

- Dead bug: supine (adapt floor-press base), contralateral reach — A both knees 90/90 +
  arms to ceiling, B right arm overhead + left leg long, hovering. Pelvis/lumbar FLAT
  throughout (s constant). Alternating type — no bil flag.
- Glute bridge: supine knees bent; B lifts pelvis to a straight shoulder–hip–knee line.
  Hip extension only; spine values stay neutral (no arch). cam side-ish.
- Half-kneeling HF stretch: A tall half-kneel → M posterior tuck (pelvis rolls under, no
  forward travel) → B small forward shift with tuck held. bil. Front shin vertical.
- DB row: hinged ~45–60° torso, neutral-grip DBs (joint-attached db props both hands),
  elbows drive up-back toward hip. swAL/swAR fit numerically per side (never mirrored).
- Bench dips: hands on bench edge beside hips (bench = prop lines as in strength app),
  feet forward on floor; elbows bend until upper arms parallel — shoulder NEVER below
  elbow (y-down: shL.y/shR.y ≤ elbow y at every phase; numeric QA + eyeball).
- Prone Y-T-W: cam 72 (Y-raise lesson), A=Y, M=T, B=W so the cycle sweeps Y→T→W→T→Y.
  Thumbs-up micro-lifts, blades down/back.
- Wall slide: back to wall (vertical prop line), goalpost forearms slide up/down.
- Farmer hold: standing tall, db prop in each hand at the sides, subtle brace/breath sway.
- Standing HF/quad stretch: one heel to glute, same-side hand holds ankle (`ikL:{k:'ftL'}`
  is safe: same joint key in A and B — never blended), knee down, posterior tuck. bil.
- Cat-cow: quadruped arch/round (adapt prenatal hands-and-knees poses), hf sweep.
- Posture reset: standing tall, ribs-over-pelvis stack, br:true breath tone, cyc ~5s.
